Website Development Cost in India: What Affects the Price?

The cost of creating a website in India can vary widely. A simple informational website and a custom online store require different amounts of planning, design and development. Instead of relying on one universal price, understand what is included in a quote.

1. Type and size of website

A basic informational website usually needs fewer features than an e-commerce store, booking platform or custom web application.

The number of unique page layouts, languages and content sections can also affect the amount of work involved.

2. Design and user experience

A website using a standard layout may take less design work than a fully customised experience. Responsive layouts, accessibility and careful testing also require time.

Ask whether the quote includes mobile layouts and revisions, and how many design changes are covered.

3. Features and integrations

Contact forms, appointment booking, payment gateways, product catalogues, user accounts and third-party integrations add complexity.

List the features you actually need. Avoid paying for functionality that does not support your business goals.

4. Content, images and SEO

Writing page content, preparing product information, sourcing images and creating original graphics may be included or charged separately.

Basic SEO setup can include page titles, descriptions, heading structure, clean URLs and sitemap preparation. Ongoing SEO work is a separate activity.

5. Domain, hosting and maintenance

Domain registration and hosting are commonly recurring expenses. Their prices depend on the provider, plan and renewal period.

Ask who manages updates, backups, security checks and technical support after launch. Confirm whether these services are included in the quote.

6. Questions to ask before accepting a quote

Ask for a written list of pages, features, deliverables, revision limits and estimated timeline. Confirm who owns the domain, hosting account, design files and website content.

Compare quotes by scope and support rather than choosing only the lowest headline price. Request clarification about recurring charges and optional services.

7. Example: Planning a five-page website for a local business

Imagine a local electrician, salon, consultant or repair service that wants a simple website. Before asking a developer for a quote, write down exactly what the business needs.

Possible website structure

  1. Home: A clear introduction, main services and a contact button.
  2. About: Business background, experience and service area.
  3. Services: Details of the work offered and common customer questions.
  4. Gallery: Genuine photographs of completed work, products or the business.
  5. Contact: Phone, email, WhatsApp link, enquiry form and address where applicable.

This is an example scope, not a fixed package. A business may need fewer pages or additional features such as appointment booking, multiple languages or a product catalogue. Each extra requirement should be listed separately in the quotation.

8. Make a website budget worksheet

Use this checklist to prepare your requirements before requesting a price. Ask the developer to show each item separately so you can understand what you are paying for.

  • Domain registration and renewal
  • Hosting plan and renewal
  • Number of pages and unique layouts
  • Website design and mobile-friendly development
  • Content writing and image preparation
  • Contact forms, WhatsApp links or booking features
  • Basic SEO setup and sitemap
  • Testing, launch and domain connection
  • Backups, maintenance and technical support

Mark each item as included, optional or not required. This makes it easier to compare two quotations with different scopes.

9. One-time costs versus recurring costs

Some website expenses are related to the initial build, while others continue after launch. The distinction matters when calculating the total cost of owning a website.

Possible one-time project costs

  • Website planning and initial design
  • Page development and agreed custom features
  • Initial content entry and image preparation
  • Testing and launch assistance

Possible recurring expenses

  • Domain renewal
  • Web hosting subscription
  • Paid themes, plugins or third-party services
  • Ongoing maintenance, backups and support

Ask who will pay each renewal and whether the accounts will be registered in your name. Check renewal prices, not only introductory offers.

10. How to compare website development quotations

Do not compare quotations by the final amount alone. One developer may include content, hosting setup and revisions, while another may charge for those separately.

Ask every developer the same questions:

  1. How many pages and features are included?
  2. Will the website work on mobile phones and tablets?
  3. Who will provide the text, photographs and other content?
  4. How many design revisions are included?
  5. Will I own the domain, hosting account and website files?
  6. Are SSL, contact forms, basic SEO and sitemap setup included?
  7. What are the renewal, maintenance and support charges?
  8. What happens if I want additional pages or features later?

Request a written scope, delivery timeline and payment schedule. Keep a copy of the final agreement and account details.

11. Common website budget mistakes

  • Choosing only by the lowest quote: Check what is excluded before making a decision.
  • Ignoring renewals: A low first-year offer may have different renewal charges.
  • Adding unnecessary features: Start with what customers actually need.
  • Not checking ownership: Make sure you can access your domain, hosting and website files.
  • Assuming SEO guarantees rankings: Basic technical setup does not guarantee search traffic or a particular Google position.
  • Forgetting maintenance: Decide who will handle updates, backups and troubleshooting after launch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there one fixed price for every website in India?

No. A simple informational website, an online store and a custom web application require different levels of planning and development. Compare the included scope before comparing prices.

Are domain and hosting one-time costs?

Usually not. Domain registration is renewed periodically, and hosting is commonly billed monthly or annually. Check the renewal terms with the provider.

What should a website quote include?

It should list the pages, features, design work, content responsibilities, revision limits, delivery timeline, payment terms, ownership, exclusions and recurring costs.
