Small Business SEO: Practical Basics

Search engine optimisation (SEO) helps search engines understand website content and helps people find useful pages. SEO is an ongoing process, not a one-time setting or a guarantee of ranking.

1. Start with your customers' questions

Think about what customers search for before choosing a product or service. Create pages that answer those questions clearly and accurately.

Focus each page on a useful topic. Avoid repeating keywords unnaturally or creating many near-identical pages.

2. Write clear page titles and descriptions

Give every important page a unique, descriptive title. A good title tells visitors what the page is about without unnecessary repetition.

Write a concise meta description that accurately summarises the page. Search engines may choose a different snippet for some searches.

3. Organise content with headings and links

Use a clear main heading and descriptive subheadings. Break long topics into sections so readers can find information quickly.

Link related pages together using meaningful link text. Internal links help visitors discover relevant information and help search engines understand site structure.

4. Make the website technically accessible

Check that important pages load, use HTTPS and work on mobile devices. Avoid broken links and make sure navigation is understandable.

Create an XML sitemap and submit it through Google Search Console. A sitemap can help search engines discover URLs, but it does not guarantee indexing.

5. Improve local business information

If your business serves a local area, keep its name, address, phone number and opening hours accurate wherever you publish them.

A complete Google Business Profile can help eligible local businesses appear in Google Search and Maps. Keep details current and respond professionally to customer feedback.

6. Measure and improve over time

Use Google Search Console to review search queries, clicks, impressions and indexing issues. Use analytics tools where appropriate to understand what visitors do on your site.

Update pages when information changes. Prioritise accuracy and usefulness rather than publishing content only to increase page count.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does SEO take?

There is no guaranteed timeline. Results depend on competition, website quality, content, technical factors and how search engines process changes.

Does adding keywords guarantee a high ranking?

No. Keywords alone do not guarantee rankings. Content should answer the visitor's question and be supported by a technically accessible website.

Is a sitemap enough to get pages indexed?

No. A sitemap can help search engines discover URLs, but indexing is not guaranteed.
