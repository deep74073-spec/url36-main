How to Plan a Business Website

A useful business website helps visitors understand what you offer, decide whether it is right for them and contact you without confusion. Planning the site before designing it can save time and prevent unnecessary pages.

1. Decide what the website should achieve

Start with the main purpose. Do you want customers to call, request a quote, book an appointment, visit your shop or learn about your services? Choose one primary action and make it easy to find.

Think about the people who will visit the website. List the questions they are likely to ask before choosing your business.

2. Choose the essential pages

Most small business websites benefit from a clear Home page, About page, Services or Products page and Contact page. Add a FAQ, portfolio or booking page only when it helps visitors make a decision.

Give every page a clear purpose. Avoid adding pages just to make the website look bigger.

3. Prepare useful content

Write a short explanation of what your business does, who it serves and what makes the service useful. Use plain language rather than complicated marketing phrases.

Use real photographs of your business, products or work when possible. Do not publish invented reviews, client logos or project results.

4. Make contact details easy to find

Show the correct phone number, email address and service area where relevant. Test contact forms on a phone and desktop before publishing.

If customers visit a physical location, provide accurate address and opening-hour information.

5. Check the mobile experience

Many visitors use phones. Check that text is readable, buttons are easy to tap, images fit the screen and important information is not hidden.

Test the website on a real mobile device and a slower internet connection.

6. Review before launch

Check every link, spelling, page title, description and contact method. Make sure the website uses HTTPS and that visitors can reach the important pages from the navigation.

After launch, review visitor questions and update the content when your services or business information change.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many pages does a small business website need?

There is no fixed number. Start with the pages visitors need to understand your business and contact you. Add more only when they provide useful information.

Should a business website work on mobile phones?

Yes. A mobile-friendly layout makes content easier to read and actions easier to use on smaller screens.

What should the homepage contain?

A clear explanation of the business, its main services, useful next steps and an easy way to make contact.
