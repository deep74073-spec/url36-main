About URL36

URL36 provides website design and development services for businesses, professionals, creators and individuals who want to build or improve their online presence.

What We Can Build

  • Business websites for shops, clinics, restaurants, schools and service providers.
  • Personal websites, portfolios and online resumes.
  • Landing pages for products, services and campaigns.
  • Blogs, article websites and content websites.
  • Product catalogue websites with enquiry and WhatsApp contact options.
  • Affiliate websites, product reviews and comparison websites.
  • Online tools such as calculators, converters and other browser-based utilities.

Website Improvements and Support

  • Website redesign and mobile-friendly layout improvements.
  • Basic SEO setup, including page titles, descriptions, sitemap and robots.txt.
  • Domain connection, website deployment and launch assistance.
  • Content updates and basic website maintenance.

How We Work

We first discuss your purpose, required pages, content and features. The work and estimate depend on the project scope. Domain registration, hosting, paid themes, plugins and third-party services may involve additional costs.

Advanced requirements such as online payments, customer accounts, booking systems and custom applications need separate discussion and estimates.
